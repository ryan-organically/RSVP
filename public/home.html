      The science · ORP
      
Your eyes don't
        read words. They fixate on them.

      
Normal reading burns time on
        saccades — the tiny jumps your eyes make between words. Focal removes the jumps entirely: it flashes
        one word at a time and pins each word's Optimal Recognition Point to the
        exact same spot. Your eye never moves. Recognition becomes instant.

      
01
Zero saccades
No left-to-right scanning — the words come to you.

      
02
Pivot-aligned
The red letter is the ORP, locked to the crosshair on every word.

      
03
Full comprehension
Your brain recognizes the whole word from its pivot — faster, not shallower.
